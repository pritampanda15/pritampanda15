## About Me

I’m an **AI for Science & Computational Drug Discovery Scientist** at **Stanford University School of Medicine** with **8+ years** building scalable scientific machine learning systems across protein foundation models, **SE(3)-equivariant & generative molecular ML**, GPU-accelerated docking, **alchemical free-energy (FEP)** engines, **virtual-cell CRISPRi perturbation models**, and **self-evaluating agentic bio-AI harnesses**.

- **Translational Scale:** Scaled closed-loop active learning across **2.5B+ molecules** (AutoDock-GPU, Glide, Boltz-2, ADMET) to advance a **DoD/DARPA** therapeutic program into **Phase II clinical trials**, cutting wet-lab testing by **60%** and helping secure **$8M+** in research funding.

- **Agentic Scientific Engineering:** Built & deployed **2,000+ Claude/Gemini agentic bio-skills** (**Biomni + Antigravity**) automating AlphaFold2/3, FEP, MD, R-group design, and clinical workflows — backed by **AutoHarness** automated regression eval loops.
- **Scholarship & Open Source:** Creator of **15 open-source tools (PandaVerse)**; author of **89 Q1 papers** (*Science Advances*, *Nature Medicine*, *Cell Death & Disease*, *ACS Omega*, *Leukemia*; **6,560+ citations**, **h-index 49**, **i10-index 105**) and co-inventor on **1 U.S. patent application** (`US20250339544A1`).
